Why it matters

Your people are your biggest cost and your biggest risk.

Most owners of small and medium businesses handle HR themselves, between everything else. It works until a dismissal goes wrong, a key person resigns, or a team stops pulling together. Having an HR person in your corner changes that.

  • Better staffHiring against a clear job description and the competencies the role needs, not gut feel.
  • More productive staffPerformance reviews and incentives tied to outcomes, so people know what good looks like.
  • A team that works togetherStructure, clear roles and conflict dealt with early, before it costs you a resignation.
  • An extra set of eyesEvery contract, warning and policy checked against the BCEA, LRA and EEA before it goes out.
  • An extra set of earsSomeone staff can raise problems with, so you hear about them while they're still small.

AI can draft a policy. It can't sit across the table from an upset employee. That's HR, and that's what we do.

    Questions

    What owners usually ask first.

    How is this different from an HR software subscription?

    Software stores records and generates templates. It can't run a disciplinary hearing, coach a new manager or tell you whether a dismissal will hold. You get a person who does those things, and who uses whatever systems you already have.

    Do you work on site or remotely?

    Both. Most clients have a regular on-site day and reach their HR executive by phone, email or WhatsApp in between.

    What happens if we have a CCMA case?

    Your HR executive prepares the case with you, from documentation to witness preparation, and our employee relations specialists can represent you where the rules allow.

    Are we locked into a long contract?

    No. Retainers run month to month. Start with the health check, and continue only if the support is earning its keep.

    Can you still help us recruit?

    Yes. Recruitment is part of the same service, so the person who wrote the job description is the one finding the candidates.
