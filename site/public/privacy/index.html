Privacy

Privacy notice

This notice explains how Intrinsic Consulting Solutions (ICS) handles the personal information you give us through this website, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

What we collect

When you book an HR Health Check, contact us or request a checklist, we collect your name, business name, email address, phone number, the number of people you employ and anything you choose to tell us in your message.

Why we collect it

We use it to respond to your enquiry, arrange and deliver your HR Health Check, and send you the resources you ask for. We'll only send you other updates if you agree to receive them, and you can opt out at any time.

Who sees it

Your information is seen by the ICS team. We use trusted service providers to run this website and our email, and they may only use your information on our instructions. We don't sell your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry or provide our services, or as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent. Email admin@icsol.co.za.

Candidates and job seekers

If you apply for a role or send us your CV, our POPI policy for candidates applies.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, contact us first at admin@icsol.co.za. You can also complain to the Information Regulator: https://inforegulator.org.za
